## Supplier candidates (probe list — verify size/color and MOQ)

- Heated insoles: aliexpress search `heated insoles rechargeable` (look for 2-pair USB-C kits, 3-level)
- Travel mug: `double wall travel mug 480ml` (verify twist-lock lid)
- Scraper mitt: `snow scraper glove` (hardened edge, cuff)
- Under-sink shelf: `under sink organizer 2 tier` (adjustable around pipe)
- Slide-out drawer: `pull out cabinet drawer adhesive` (12 kg rails)
- Spice riser: `spice rack riser 3 tier` (stackable)
- Booster seat: `dog booster car seat small` (under-9 kg)
- Pet mat: `self warming pet mat` (foil layer, washable cover)
- Dog vest: `dog winter vest fleece harness slot` (S–L)

## Hardening rules before first ad dollar

1. Order **one sample per niche** (the planned sample budget). Photograph it yourself.
2. Confirm supplier's dispatch SLA in days and its real tracking grade.
3. Set a stock cap of 40/order-day/sku for the first week; over-cap orders get an
   honest "ships in X" email, never a silent wait.
4. Free-shipping thresholds are already bundled into price — never add surprise fees.
