Walking a dog in horizontal rain without a wet car

The towel lives by the door, not in the cupboard. That one change does more than any gadget.

A dog shakes roughly 70% of the water off in the first second after a wet walk, and most of it lands within two metres of the door. Position decides whether that is the porch or the boot.

Microfibre stow towels absorb more per gram and dry faster than cotton; cotton hangs damp for hours and smells of the walk.

Dry legs and belly first, then the back against the grain. A clip torch on the lead keeps you visible on the dark side of the lane, which is the part people forget when buying for rain.
