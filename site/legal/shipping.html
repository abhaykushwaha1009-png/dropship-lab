Shipping

Every order ships free with tracking. We dispatch within one business day; the factory prints the label and hands to the carrier within three.

Times

United States and United Kingdom: 6–12 business days. Switzerland and rest of Europe: 7–14 business days — parcels go by air and clear customs there.

Tracking

The tracking number lands in the email you checked out with, usually within three days of payment. If a carrier link shows "awaiting scan" for more than 4 days, reply to your receipt and a person looks at it.

Where we ship

United States, United Kingdom and Switzerland. If your country isn't listed on the checkout page, that order can't be placed — better than taking one we can't fulfil.

Duties

Prices include shipping, not import tax. Swiss orders below CHF 62 are duty-paid in practice; above that the carrier may collect Swiss VAT on delivery.
