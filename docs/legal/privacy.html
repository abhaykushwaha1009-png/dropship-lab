Privacy

We collect three things: the email you check out with, your shipping address and the order itself. Payment card data never touches this site — checkout runs on the processor's hosted page.

Who sees it

Us, the payment processor, and the print-on-demand factory that ships your parcel. Nobody else. We don't sell data, build ad profiles or send unsolicited mail.

Cookies

This shop sets none of its own. The processor's checkout sets what it needs to take a payment and protect the transaction.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of what we hold or its deletion at any time by replying to your receipt. Orders are kept as long as tax law requires, usually six years.
