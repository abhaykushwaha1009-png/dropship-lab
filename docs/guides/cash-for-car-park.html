Getting the cash out of a scuffed car park

Two scuffs and a kerbed alloy will cost more at resale than a full detail. Fix them or the buyer discounts twice over.

Trade-in valuations dock around 120 to 200 pounds for visible paint damage, and the deduction is applied per panel rather than per mark.

A scratch that you cannot feel with a fingernail is in the clearcoat and will usually polish out. If it catches your nail, it needs touch-up paint before polishing, or the pad just rounds the edges of the gouge.

Work in shade on a cool panel, and finish with a sealant — bare polished clearcoat oxidises faster than the paint around it, so an unsealed repair looks worse in a month.

Alloy kerbing is the one to leave to a mobile repairer unless the wheel is bare metal and uncoated.
