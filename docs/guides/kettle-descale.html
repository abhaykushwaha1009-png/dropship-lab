How often a kettle actually needs descaling

Hard water areas: every four to six weeks. Soft water: every three months. Never 'when it looks bad'.

Limescale is calcium carbonate. It insulates the element, so a scaled kettle takes longer to boil and pulls more current for the same cup — the cost shows up on the bill before the kettle dies.

Descale with citric acid, not vinegar. Citric rinses clean in one pass; vinegar leaves an acetic tang that taints the next three brews.

Half fill, two tablespoons of citric acid, boil, stand twenty minutes, rinse twice, then boil fresh water once and pour it away.

If your area is above 200mg/l hardness, treat four weeks as the interval and put it in the calendar rather than trusting your memory.
