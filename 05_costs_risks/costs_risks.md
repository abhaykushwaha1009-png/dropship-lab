# Costs & Risks — AI Dropshipping Challenge

## Budget Breakdown ($300 Cap)

| Item | Estimated Cost | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Shopify plan | $29 (1st month) or $1 trial | Referenced in video |
| Domain | $0–$20 | Optional, can use myshopify.com |
| App subscriptions | $0–$50 | AutoDS, Omnisend, etc. |
| Ad spend (Facebook/TikTok) | ~$200–$250 | Remaining budget after fixed costs |
| Product samples | $0 | Optional, not in challenge |
| **Total** | **~$300** | At cap |

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| No sales in 7 days | High | Loss of $300 | Set stop-loss at $150 ad spend |
| High CPC/CPA | Medium | Budget burns fast | Start with $5–$10/day test ads |
| Product doesn't convert | High | Wasted ad spend | Test 1–3 products, kill losers fast |
| Ad account banned | Low | Campaign halted | Follow platform policies, don't over-aggress |
| Payment hold | Low | Cash flow issue | Use Stripe/PayPal, monitor holds |
| Supplier delays | Medium | Poor customer experience | Order sample, check shipping times |

## Reality Checks (from videos)
- Dropshipping is NOT get-rich-quick; it's a real business
- $1,000 in 7 days is a challenge goal, not a guarantee
- Most new stores don't hit $1,000 in week one
- Ad costs have risen 20%+ YoY (per channel content)
- Organic reach is limited; paid ads are usually needed

## Stop Conditions (Owner Decides)
- [ ] Ad spend hits $150 with zero sales → pause and reassess
- [ ] CPA > product profit margin → stop ads
- [ ] Ad account restricted → investigate before continuing
- [ ] Day 3 with no add-to-cart → pivot product or niche
