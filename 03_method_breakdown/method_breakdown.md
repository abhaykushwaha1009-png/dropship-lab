## 7-Day Execution Method

### Day 1: Niche Selection & Market Research
- **Action:** Select niche at random (from audience suggestions or product research tool)
- **AI role:** Identify trending products, analyze competition
- **Tools:** Product research marketplace/tool (cheat sheet referenced in video)
- **Output:** Selected niche + shortlist of candidate products

### Day 2: Product Selection & Store Build
- **Action:** Choose winning product(s) using cheat sheet criteria
- **AI role:** Build Shopify store automatically
- **Tools:** AI store builder (buildyourstore.ai referenced), Shopify
- **Output:** Live store with selected products

### Day 3: Branding
- **Action:** Build brand identity (name, logo, colors, messaging)
- **AI role:** Generate brand assets and guidelines
- **Tools:** AI branding tools
- **Output:** Brand kit applied to store

### Day 4: Product Import & Optimization
- **Action:** Import products, write descriptions, set pricing
- **AI role:** Write product descriptions, optimize listings
- **Tools:** AI copywriting, product import tools (AutoDS referenced)
- **Output:** Fully listed product catalog

### Day 5: Advertising Setup
- **Action:** Create and launch ad campaigns
- **AI role:** Generate ad creatives (UGC-style ads, copy, visuals)
- **Tools:** AI ad creator, Facebook/TikTok ads
- **Output:** Active ad campaigns

### Day 6: Sales Review & Optimization
- **Action:** Review sales data, identify winning/losing products
- **AI role:** Analyze data, suggest optimizations
- **Tools:** Analytics, AI data review
- **Output:** Optimized product mix and ad spend

### Day 7: Final Profit Review
- **Action:** Calculate total revenue, profit, ROI against $300 budget
- **AI role:** Profit analysis and scaling recommendations
- **Output:** Final P&L, decision to continue or pivot

## Key Principles from Videos
- **Budget discipline:** Max $300 spend (or $100–$1,000 depending on variant)
- **Copyable format:** Every step shown, nothing held back
- **Random niche:** Proves method works regardless of niche
- **AI-first:** Product research, store build, branding, ads all AI-driven
- **Transparent results:** Real sales/profit numbers shared
