# Checklist — AI Dropshipping 7-Day Challenge

## Pre-Start (Owner)
- [ ] Shopify account created
- [ ] Payment processor (Stripe/PayPal) connected
- [ ] Ad accounts ready (Facebook Business Manager, TikTok Ads)
- [ ] Supplier relationship confirmed (AliExpress/AutoDS/Teemdrop)
- [ ] $300 budget allocated and accessible
- [ ] Product research cheat sheet downloaded (from video description)
- [ ] Execution log folder created: `04_execution_plan/`

## Day 1 — Niche & Research
- [ ] Niche selected (random or from suggestions)
- [ ] Product research completed
- [ ] Winning product shortlist (1–3 items)
- [ ] Cheat sheet criteria applied to each candidate
- [ ] Notes saved to `04_execution_plan/day1_niche.md`

## Day 2 — Store Build
- [ ] Shopify store live
- [ ] AI store builder used
- [ ] Basic settings configured (payments, shipping, taxes)
- [ ] Store URL recorded

## Day 3 — Branding
- [ ] Brand name chosen
- [ ] Logo generated (AI)
- [ ] Colors/fonts applied to theme
- [ ] Legal pages written (About, Contact, Refund, Privacy)

## Day 4 — Products
- [ ] 1–3 winning products imported
- [ ] AI-written titles and descriptions
- [ ] Pricing set with margin for ad budget
- [ ] Product pages live and checkout working

## Day 5 — Ads
- [ ] Ad account funded (within remaining budget)
- [ ] AI ad creatives generated
- [ ] Test campaigns launched ($50–$100/day)
- [ ] Tracking pixel installed

## Day 6 — Optimize
- [ ] Sales data reviewed
- [ ] Losing products/ads paused
- [ ] Winning products scaled
- [ ] Optimization notes saved

## Day 7 — Final Review
- [ ] Total revenue calculated
- [ ] Total costs tallied (ads + Shopify + apps)
- [ ] Profit = Revenue − Costs
- [ ] vs $1,000 goal: [ ] Hit [ ] Missed
- [ ] Decision: [ ] Scale [ ] Pivot [ ] Stop
- [ ] Final P&L saved to `04_execution_plan/day7_pnl.md`
